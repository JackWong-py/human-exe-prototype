# AI-drafted replies
Safety design: facts come from the pipeline; the AI only rewords; every answer is checked; a person sends it; results never change.
Cases covered: missing attachment, wrong document, unreadable file, blank value, defect.
Real-model test: 12 emails tried; 12 AI drafts accepted, 0 rejected.
Limits: English only; tested on 12 emails; the wording will still needs a person's review for safety.
